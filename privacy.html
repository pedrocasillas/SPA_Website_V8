Privacy

How your information is used.

This notice explains what happens to the information you provide through the Startup Pitch Advisory website.

Information collected

When you use the contact form, the information collected may include your name, email address, startup or organisation, and the details you include in your message.

Why it is used

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, understand what you are preparing for and, where appropriate, discuss possible work with you.

Sharing and storage

Your enquiry may be processed by the services used to host the website, deliver the form and manage email. Your information is not sold. It is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, manage any resulting working relationship and meet applicable legal or record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information is held about you, request a correction or deletion, or object to its use where applicable. You can make a request through the contact page.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question about how your information is used, contact Pedro through the website. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk ↗.
