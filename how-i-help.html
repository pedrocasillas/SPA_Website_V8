How I help

Start with the level of help you need.

Sometimes you need an independent diagnosis. Sometimes you need focused preparation. Sometimes you need support across several investor conversations.

01 · Recommended starting point

Investor Readiness Review

An independent assessment of the pitch you have now: what is clear, what is missing and what deserves attention before the next investor conversation.

You receive

  • A written assessment of the business explanation, story and likely investor questions
  • The three most important changes to make first
  • Questions and assumptions to prepare for
  • A feedback conversation about the findings and next steps

Clear boundaries

The review does not provide investment, introductions, financial due diligence or a guarantee of funding.

Scope, timing and fee are agreed before work begins.

02 · Focused work

Pitch & Q&A Preparation

Strengthen the part that needs attention: story, structure, evidence, explanation, difficult questions or preparation for the room.

  • Clarify the central investment argument
  • Make complex explanations easier to follow
  • Test the evidence behind important claims
  • Prepare for questions about traction, economics and risk
  • Rehearse the actual investor conversation
03 · Continuing support

Ongoing Pitch Advisory

For founders who want an independent communication adviser across several investor conversations.

Review investor feedback, decide what to change, refine the case and prepare for the next meeting without rebuilding everything after every reaction.

Common questions

Before you enquire

Do I need a finished deck?

No. A draft deck, written notes or a spoken explanation can provide enough material to identify where clarity is breaking down.

Will you redesign my slides?

The work focuses first on the argument and its communication. If slide communication is part of the problem, that can be included in the agreed scope.

Will you judge whether the business is investable?

I assess how clearly and credibly the founder communicates the case. The work is not financial due diligence or an investment recommendation.

Which conversation are you preparing for?

Describe the raise, your current pitch and the help you think you need.
