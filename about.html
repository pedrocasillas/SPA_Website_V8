My perspective

The founder knows the business. The investor still needs a clear case.

For more than two decades, I have helped people explain important ideas, structure arguments and communicate under pressure.

I created Startup Pitch Advisory after watching promising founders lose clarity in the investor room—some buried the opportunity in detail; others had a polished deck but weak answers underneath it.

My role is to test whether the argument can be followed, whether important claims are supported and whether the founder can defend the case when questions begin.
