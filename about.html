My perspective

The founder knows the business. The investor still needs a clear case.

For more than 25 years, I have helped people structure important messages, question assumptions and communicate under pressure.

I created Startup Pitch Advisory after watching promising founders bury the opportunity in detail—or arrive with polished slides and weak answers underneath them.

My role is to test whether the argument can be followed, whether important claims are supported and whether the founder can defend the case when the questions begin.
