A defined role

Clearer communication, not an investment recommendation.

I test how well the founder communicates the business, evidence, raise and assumptions. I do not provide investment, make investor introductions, perform financial due diligence or certify a business as investable.

What should be different afterwards?

The founder should be able to explain the opportunity more clearly, identify where evidence is weak, state the raise and use of capital directly, and respond more effectively to investor questions.
